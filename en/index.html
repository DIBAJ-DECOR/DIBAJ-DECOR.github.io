About the Company

At Dibaj Decor Company, we offer many luxurious and modern products that satisfy all tastes, including: roller blinds, luxury curtain fabrics, medical curtains, wooden blinds, zebra blinds, vertical blinds, metal blinds, Roman blinds, electric blinds, skylight shades, movable shades, car shades, shutters, smart home with all accessories and electric motors, accordion doors, in addition to parquet, wallpaper, wood alternative, marble alternative, and some interior finishing works.

We rely in our work on a long history of experience, high efficiency and mastery in installation and delivery of projects, where we practice our work professionally and with high professionalism, in addition to after-sales maintenance for all our products if needed, by trained hands with long experience in this specialization.
