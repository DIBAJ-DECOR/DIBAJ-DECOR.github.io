About Dibaj Decor

Crafting luxury interiors in the heart of Jeddah.

Dibaj Decor is a luxury interior design studio based in Jeddah, dedicated to transforming residential and commercial spaces into timeless experiences. With over 10 years of expertise, we blend contemporary elegance with authentic Arabian warmth.

Every project starts with listening. We study light, proportion, and your daily rituals, then layer natural stone, brushed brass, oak, and bespoke joinery to create spaces that feel both elevated and deeply personal.

From concept to final finishing, our team manages design, 3D visualization, material selection, and site supervision — ensuring flawless execution.
