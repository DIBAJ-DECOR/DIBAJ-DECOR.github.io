• About The Company

At DIBAJ Decor, we offer a wide range of luxurious and modern products to satisfy all tastes: Roller blinds, luxury curtain fabrics, medical curtains, wooden blinds, zebra blinds, vertical blinds, metal blinds, Roman blinds, electric pergolas, retractable glass roof awnings, car shades, shutters, smart home systems with all accessories and electric motors, plus accordion doors, parquet flooring, wallpaper, wood alternative, marble alternative and some interior finishing works. We rely on a long history of experience, high efficiency, product quality and precision in installation and project delivery, working with professionalism and following up maintenance for all our products with trained and highly experienced hands.

• Company Goal

DIBAJ Decor aims to meet the needs of the local market with different types of curtains, awnings and decorations, offering the latest global creations in beautiful colors and attractive designs that match the desires and interests of our customers.

• Company Message

DIBAJ Decor defines its mission through customer satisfaction, by giving them elegant and luxurious designs that exceed their expectations with guaranteed quality, making them feel satisfied, which increases our determination to keep up with development to offer our services with the finest products, latest technologies and highest quality standards.
